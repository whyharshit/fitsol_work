key aspects, engagement with stakeholders, and content, preparation and presentation of the Identiﬁed Sustainability 
Information in accordance with the Criteria. This responsibility includes design, implementation, and maintenance of internal 
control relevant to the preparation of the BRSR, and the measurement of Identiﬁed Sustainability Information, which is free 
from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error. The Management and the Board of Directors of the Company 
are also responsible for overseeing the Company’s compliance with the requirements of LODR Regulations and the SEBI 
Circulars in relation to the BRSR Core.
Inherent Limitations in preparing the Identiﬁed Sustainability Information
The absence of a signiﬁcant body of established practice on which to draw to evaluate and measure non-ﬁnancial 
information allows for different, but acceptable, measures and measurement techniques and can affect comparability
