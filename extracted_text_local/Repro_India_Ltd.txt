2.	
Steps taken to inform and educate consumers about safe and 
responsible usage of products and/or services.
	
Print on demand business solutions are designed to minimise raw material 
wastage as well as promote zero wastages of finished goods due to 
obsolescence. Customers are encouraged to adopt repro offerings that 
uphold the spirit of sustainability as well as curb the possibilities of product 
piracy. Repro tamper proof packaging ensures that products are delivered 
safely and transit pilferages if any are reported for corrective actions.

agreement.
