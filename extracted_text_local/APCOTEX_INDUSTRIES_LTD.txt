3.	
	
Describe the processes in place to safely reclaim your products for reusing, recycling, and disposing at the 
end of life, for (a) Plastics (including packaging) (b) E-waste (c) Hazardous waste and (d) other waste. 
	
	
The Company does not reclaim any products for reusing, recycling, and disposing at the end of life. Considering our 
nature of business, reclamation of products from customers and consumers after useful life is not material and feasible. 
Furthermore, reclaiming packaging materials from customers has operational challenges due to diverse industries. We
