2)	
Installed an in-house solar power system, capable of generating 1,600 kWh/day – completed in March 2024.

Achieved a 50% reduction in Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions in FY 2024-25.

Received ISO 45001 (Occupational Health and Safety) certification for all facilities – December 2024.
5)	
Received ISO 14001:2015 (Environmental Management System) certification for all facilities – December 2024.
6)	
Replaced three harmonic filters in Units 2 and 7, resulting in a CO2 saving of 23 tons – June 2024.
7)	
Replaced 20 tons of ODS gas air conditioners – September 2024.
8)	
Replaced diesel vehicles with electric shuttle vehicles for internal employee transportation – December 2024.
9)	
Reduced solder bar consumption by 3.4 tons/year through improved material usage control – March 2025.
10)	
Installed EV charging stations in parking areas to promote electric vehicle usage among employees – August 2024.
11)	
Eliminated thermocol in BLDC motor packaging by switching to green packaging materials.
12)	
Reduced plastic runner waste by 9 tons/year through improved tool design – March 2025.
13)	
Reduced end bit shaft scrap by 2.7 tons/year – February 2025.
14)	
Reduced brass scrap by 0.5 tons/year through tool design improvements – January 2025.
