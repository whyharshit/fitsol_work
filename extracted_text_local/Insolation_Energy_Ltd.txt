which reaŨrms our deep commitment to sustainability and to the principles of Environmental, Social, and 
Governance (ESG) principles.
Environmental Responsibility: At Insolation, we understand the critical importance of minimizing our ecological 
footprint and actively contributing to a sustainable future. As a renewable energy company, our core business 
model is inherently aligned with environmental stewardship. However, we continuously strive to enhance our 
environmental performance through innovation, eŨciency, and responsible resource management. 
Social Responsibility: Our engagement with local communities has long been a cornerstone of our growth

and compliance standards. Looking ahead, we are committed to continual improvement to raise the bar even 
higher.

sustainability and are conﬁdent that, together with your continued support, we can pave the way for a greener, 
more inclusive, and responsible future for generations to come.
