Describe the processes in place to safely reclaim your products for reusing, recycling and disposing at the end of life, for (a)

We believe in "waste to wealth” and from the very beginning of the project and manufacturing process, we adopted the “Reduce -
Reuse - Recycle (3Rs) " principle. UCWL is utilizing recycled material from other industrial waste. The company follows circularity 
principles in its all stages of manufacturing.

Responsibility (EPR) under PWM Rules 2016 & as amended. As per the EPR guidelines, we have completed 100% target for 
FY 2023-24. The major product of UCWL is Cement, which is packed in cement bags. Plastic packaging bags are also 
recycled by authorized recyclers. 
 
b) 
E-waste:- There is no E-waste generated from the manufacturing process. However, the only E-waste generated is from the 
ofﬁce operations and E-Waste generated is stored at designated places & sold to the CPCB registered recyclers.
