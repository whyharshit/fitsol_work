RailTel Corporation of India Limited is dedicated to 
incorporating Environmental, Social, and Governance 
(ESG) principles into its operations. 
RailTel is tackling the challenge of optimizing energy 
consumption across its extensive telecommunications 
infrastructure, with ambitious goals to enhance 
efficiency and reduce carbon emissions through 
advanced technologies. In FY 2024–25 we achieved 
milestones including ISO 14001:2015 certification 
and expanded telepresence and E-office services

risk management. The Akansha Super-30 initiative

progress and foster enduring stakeholder confidence.
