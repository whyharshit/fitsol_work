c. 	
A brief on types of customers
	
	
RR Kabel is one of the largest manufacturers of Wires and Cables, and a prominent player in the Fast-Moving 
Electrical Goods (FMEG) space. RR Kabel is also one of the largest exporters of cables in India, serving customers 
across various sectors such globally. Spread across multiple business verticals including Wires & Cables, Switches, 
Fans, Lighting, Switchgears & Appliances, we continue to endeavour to create top-quality products using futuristic 
advances in design and engineering. We offer an extensive range of products used for residential, commercial, 
industrial, and infrastructure purposes. As one of India’s largest cable exporters, RR Kabel serves a diverse global 
customer base across various sectors.
