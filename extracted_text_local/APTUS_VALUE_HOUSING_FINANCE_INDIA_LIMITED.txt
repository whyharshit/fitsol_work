a.	 Does the entity have procedures in place for sustainable sourcing? (Yes/No)

provision of financial solutions rather than the use of physical raw materials. Unlike manufacturing or 
resource-intensive industries, our business does not involve procurement of goods or natural resources 
that would typically warrant sustainable sourcing practices. However, we remain committed to
