Sharda Cropchem’s core activities focus on product registration, marketing, and distribution of agrochemical products.

other waste is minimal. Any waste produced is responsibly managed, with materials sold to authorised local vendors 
for recycling or proper disposal. Sharda Cropchem also promotes the use of recyclable paper and is committed to 
reducing paper consumption whenever possible. Given the nature of its operations, waste reclamation processes are
