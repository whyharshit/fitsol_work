Statement by director responsible for the business responsibility report, highlighting ESG related challenges, 
targets and achievements:
	
At MHRIL, we recognize that communities, the environment and our people are central to our long-term business 
continuity. Guided by our commitment to Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) principles, we integrate 
sustainability into our strategic decision-making. FY 2024–25 marked another year of significant progress, learning 
and impact as we aligned our business goals with the expectations outlined in the Business Responsibility and 
Sustainability Report (“BRSR”) framework. Regulatory Adaptation: Keeping pace with evolving ESG and BRSR 
regulatory expectations remained an ongoing challenge. We responded with continuous internal alignment and 
stakeholder education. Value Chain Engagement: Ensuring that our suppliers and partners understood and adhered 
to BRSR-aligned practices required persistent collaboration and capacity-building. Cross-Functional Data Integration: 
Coordinating ESG data across departments (engineering, finance, procurement, legal and operations) to create a 
single source of truth was a complex but necessary exercise. 
	
Targets Set:
•	 RE100 by 2050: MHRIL continues to progress toward sourcing 100% renewable electricity across all resorts.
•	 EP100 by 2030: Aiming to double energy productivity through improved efficiency and innovation. 
•	 Carbon Neutrality by 2040: Our decarbonization strategy remains aligned with science-based targets.
•	 SBTi Commitment: Achieving an 88.3% GHG reduction by 2031 in line with our approved near-term targets.
	
Education Support: Sponsored education for 3,500 girls during the year 2024-25. 
	
Afforestation Initiatives: Planted 25,934 saplings, contributing to a cumulative total of 5,65,544 trees planted since 2011. 
	
Sustainable Resort Practices: Our Virajpet resort in Coorg remains India’s first certified Zero Waste to Landfill resort, 
setting a benchmark for responsible tourism.
	
MHRIL's Sustainability Framework: 'Planet Positive' anchored on three key pillars: greening operations, de-carbonizing 
the supply chain and rejuvenating nature. Our sustainability strategy is shaped by our accumulated expertise and 
driven by a belief that long-term business success is inseparable from our impact on people and the planet. MHRIL 
remains steadfast in advancing ESG best practices and contributing meaningfully to a sustainable, equitable future.
