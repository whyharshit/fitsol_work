to the employer within one month.

practices and bolster employee satisfaction. This proactive approach underscores the Company’s commitment to 
fostering a harmonious work environment where grievances are swiftly and fairly addressed.

5.	 Does the entity have a business continuity and disaster management plan? Give details in 100 
words/weblink.
	
Yes, the Company has a Board-approved Business Continuity Plan (BCP) in place. This plan is essential for ensuring 
the Company’s resilience against disruptions. It outlines procedures to maintain operations during unforeseen events 
such as natural disasters, cyber-attacks, or pandemics. The BCP includes risk assessments, recovery strategies, and 
communication protocols, with a focus on key components such as data backup, alternate work sites, and emergency 
contacts. Regular testing and updates ensure the plan remains effective and relevant.
	
The Company maintains two availability zones (AZs) to house its data, ensuring seamless operations during disruptions. 
Key elements of the plan include data redundancy, automated failover, and regular backups across both AZs. The BCP 
incorporates real-time data replication to maintain consistency and minimize data loss. Regular testing of disaster 
recovery protocols ensures operational readiness. Clear communication channels and defined roles for staff during 
incidents further support resilience. By leveraging the geographic separation of AZs, the Company mitigates risks from 
localized events and ensures the continuity of critical services for clients with minimal downtime.
