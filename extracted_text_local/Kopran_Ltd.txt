operations as swiftly as possible. Speed is essential in all response actions.
Basic Objectives of the On-site Emergency Plan:
 
 
Identify the emergency and the affected area.
 
 
Communicate the type of emergency and initiate appropriate response actions.
 
 
Contain and ultimately control the incident to minimize damage to property, life, and environment.
 
 
Rescue and provide treatment to casualties while ensuring the safety of others.
