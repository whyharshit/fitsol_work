Our strong values guide our pursuit of profitability alongside responsibility to all stakeholders. 
This commitment to social good is embedded in our business philosophy. We achieve trust 
through high standards of corporate governance, including independence, accountability, 
transparency, and ethical leadership. Balancing economic goals with social, individual, and 
community needs, we consider environmental, social, and governance (ESG) factors. Our robust 
corporate governance practices are a cornerstone of our ESG strategy and vision. We operate 
in full compliance with Indian regulations and are overseen by a diverse and qualified Board of 
Directors, which sets strategic direction while ensuring the highest ethical standards. Together, 
management and the board work to maintain our reputation for integrity and excellence. 
We believe in the transformative power of Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) and integrate 
it into our core business practices. Focused on underserved communities and promoting sus-
tainable and inclusive growth, our CSR efforts center on Education and Employment. We aim to 
make a positive impact in these critical areas through dedicated initiatives and partnerships, 
contributing meaningfully to society and building a sustainable future for all.

Quarterly ESG walks are undertaken to keep a check on the effective implementation and practice of health 
and safety measures.

Maintain annual action plans to improve OHS performance.
 y

We have various processes in place to ensure a proactive approach to identify and mitigate work-related hazards, there-
by fostering a safer work environment.
Routine Hazard Identification and Risk Assessment:
 y
Conduct scheduled inspections and audits to identify potential hazards in the workplace.
 y
Implement training programs to educate employees about workplace safety, hazard identification, and risk 
assessment techniques.
 y
Establish safety committees that include representatives from various departments to regularly review and 
discuss safety concerns and hazard reports.
 y
Maintaining a systematic approach for employees to report hazards and near-miss incidents, which are then 
investigated to prevent recurrence.
Non-Routine Hazard Identification and Risk Assessment:
 y
Conduct risk assessments for non-routine tasks or activities to identify potential hazards specific to those tasks.
 y
Implement change management processes that include risk assessments when introducing new equipment,

 y
Continuously monitor safety performance metrics and reviewing the effectiveness of hazard identification and
