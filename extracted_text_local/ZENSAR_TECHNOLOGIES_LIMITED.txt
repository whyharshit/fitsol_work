Zensar is a renowned technology solutions company serving more than 166 clients across the globe. We work 
with esteemed Fortune 2000 firms across industries such as banking and financial services, insurance, hi-tech, 
manufacturing, retail, and healthcare. To enhance customer experience, reduce cost of operations and advance

Water Conservation

promote sustainability.

the potential vulnerability of its

operational areas is high, the 
likelihood of these occurrences 
remains low, given that few 
premises are situated in climate-
prone regions. Nonetheless, 
due to the rapidly changing 
nature and unforeseen impacts 
of these events, we continue to 
promote heightened awareness 
and preparedness measures 
throughout the organization.
The company has established 
a comprehensive Crisis

along with Business 
Continuity policies to

responsibilities during 
climate emergencies. Crisis

regional and functional levels 
to handle anticipated and 
unforeseen crises effectively.

sustainability goals, Zensar

scenario. We actively 
participate in initiatives like 
Transform to Net Zero and 
the World Economic Forum to 
advance these efforts.

temperatures leading to 
frequent heatwaves, our 
operations could face 
challenges like increased 
costs due to employee health 
issues, energy consumption,

systems. To address these, 
we aim to expand renewable 
energy generation to meet 
growing energy needs and 
minimize environmental 
impact.

the challenge of adhering to

compliance requirements,

adverse incidents, potentially 
leading to financial losses, 
compromising employee 
well-being, and tarnishing 
the organization’s reputation.

1 and Scope 2 emissions 
through FY25.

Yes. Zensar holds a certification for ISO 22301 for India locations, ensuring the establishment of an Organization-Level 
Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Plan. This comprehensive plan encompasses various potential disruption
