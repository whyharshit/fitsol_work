5.	
Has the entity implemented a mechanism for Zero Liquid Discharge? If yes, provide details of its 
coverage and implementation.
	
Our Jhagadia manufacturing site operates as a Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) facility. All effluent generated is treated through a 
comprehensive Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP), which includes both primary and secondary treatment processes. The treated 
effluent is then processed through an Industrial Reverse Osmosis (RO) system. The permeate obtained from the RO system is 
recycled back into the plant operations. The RO rejects are directed to a Multiple Effect Evaporator (MEE), where the resulting 
condensate is also recycled within the plant. The remaining sludge is dried and safely disposed of as inorganic waste at a 
government-authorised landfill site.
