Describe the mechanisms in place to receive and respond to consumer complaints and feedback.
	
Customer complaints are received by the Company’s employees facing customers. These are recorded and monitored 
for satisfactory resolution in Salesforce-based Customer Complaint Resolution System (CCRS). The complaints are

Steps taken to inform and educate consumers about safe and responsible usage of products and/

Triveni Turbine Limited ensures that all consumers are well-informed about the safe and responsible use of its products
