whenever there are changes in operations, the introduction of new equipment, or during incident investigations.
	
To proactively identify and manage work-related hazards, the Company adopts the following processes:
	
1.

implement suitable control measures to mitigate associated risks.
	
2.

processes to detect and manage risks effectively.

preventive measures accordingly.
	
5.	
Workplace Inspections: Periodic inspections of all work areas to identify unsafe conditions and ensure compliance with 
safety protocols.
	
6.	
Employee Feedback and Reporting: The Company encourages all employees to report any observed hazards, unsafe

7.	
Review of Incident Reports: Detailed analysis of incident reports to identify root causes and implement corrective and
