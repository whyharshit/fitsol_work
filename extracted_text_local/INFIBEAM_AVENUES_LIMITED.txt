4.	
Does the entity have an anti-corruption or anti-bribery policy? If yes, provide the details in brief and if 
available, provide a web-link to the policy. 
	
Yes, The Company believes in the conduct of its affairs in a fair and transparent manner to foster 
professionalism, honesty, integrity and ethical behaviour. We take a zero-tolerance approach to bribery and 
corruption and are committed to acting professionally, fairly and with integrity in all our business dealings 
and relationships, wherever we operate, and to implementing and enforcing effective systems to counter 
bribery. Our designated persons are prohibited from engaging in any bribery or potential bribery. This includes
