reduce, recycle and reuse. In addition, maximize recycling of waste and ensure safe and 
responsible disposal of waste through authorized vendors.
•	
Water Conservation: To continue to implement and improve water efficiency measures, such 
as rainwater harvesting, water-efficient fixtures, reuse of STP treated water, efficient irrigation 
systems, etc.

 To identify areas of reduction of the Company’s carbon footprint.

100% of wastewater for NKP is recycled and reused.

water within the campus for cooling and flushing.

level TRUE Certificate for Waste Management . Thus, the Company is committed to meeting all the requirements of LEED 
Green Building standards for waste reuse, recycling and disposal.
